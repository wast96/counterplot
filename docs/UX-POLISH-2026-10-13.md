## Review and changes

The review covered Outline, Chronology, Sequence, Refine, Choices, Characters, World, Connections, and Archive at desktop and phone sizes, plus the character, World, and connection editors. Isolated sample stories supplied populated states; no user stories were modified.

- **Readable titles:** outline title fields wrap and grow instead of hiding most of the title behind action icons. Enter saves the title, and resizing recalculates its height. Phone actions occupy a separate compact row; drag handles, selection, folding, and every existing action remain available.
- **Aligned beats:** beats advancing the same outer thread keep the same left edge after dragging. The previous measurement captured the temporary scale of the drop animation, then retained that false offset. Alignment now uses layout coordinates, independent of transforms on a beat or its containing threads.
- **Less navigation friction:** the selected story tab stays visible when returning from another page or resizing. Sequence and Chronology disable movement beyond either end of the list. Counts use singular/plural labels correctly.
- **Compact editing:** Refine puts opening/closing selection and Record a change on one row. Its headings and related-content spacing are tighter. The outline inspector groups type, plot membership, and status, with secondary tools arranged together.
- **Consistent library cards:** character blocks, World cards, and connection cards use tighter spacing. The large add-block tile becomes a compact row with the same action. The square portrait is retained.
- **Clearer dialogs:** phone dialogs place Help/Tutorials alongside the eyebrow and give the heading a full row. History and Used here have an explicit action group. Collapsible sections and field spacing are more consistent.
- **Accessibility:** editable state controls use 16px text on phones; focus indicators remain visible. A faint tray label has stronger contrast. The floating tray retains its layout, position, and behavior.

This pass changes presentation and existing-control behavior. It does not add story features, remove features, change the saved data format, or touch account/authentication behavior.
